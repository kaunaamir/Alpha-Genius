# AlphaGenius

> **An educational platform built to make learning more accessible.**
> **Status: Permanently Closed / Archived**

## 📚 About AlphaGenius

**AlphaGenius** was an online educational platform created with a simple goal: **make quality educational resources more accessible to students.**

The platform brought together learning materials in one place so students could spend less time searching for resources and more time actually learning.

AlphaGenius was designed around the idea that access to education shouldn't be unnecessarily complicated or expensive. It aimed to create a simple, student-focused experience where learners could discover educational content, study independently, and access useful resources from a single platform.

## 🎯 What Was AlphaGenius For?

AlphaGenius focused on making self-learning easier by providing students with access to:

* 📖 Educational and study resources
* 🎓 Learning materials across different subjects
* 📝 Practice and revision resources
* 🔎 A centralized platform for discovering useful educational content
* 💻 An accessible online learning experience

Rather than requiring students to search across dozens of different websites and platforms, AlphaGenius attempted to bring relevant resources together into one place.

## 💡 The Idea

The project was built around a straightforward philosophy:

> **Education should be accessible, discoverable, and easy to use.**

AlphaGenius wasn't intended to replace schools, teachers, or established educational platforms. Its purpose was to act as an additional resource for students who wanted to learn independently and explore material beyond their regular classroom experience.

## 🛠️ The Project

AlphaGenius grew from a small idea into a functioning online platform used by students.

The project involved much more than simply building a website. It included the development of the platform itself, organization and presentation of educational resources, user experience, infrastructure, and maintaining a service that could be accessed by students online.

This repository contains code and/or resources associated with the project and is preserved as part of AlphaGenius's development history.

## 🕊️ Why Was AlphaGenius Closed?

AlphaGenius has **ceased all operations and is permanently closed**.

The platform was discontinued following **legal action relating to the project and the educational content made available through it**. As a result, the service was taken offline and active development was stopped.

This repository remains available strictly as an **archive of the project**.

## ⚠️ Current Status

AlphaGenius is no longer an active service.

* ❌ The platform is no longer operational
* ❌ No new features are being developed
* ❌ No user services are being provided
* ❌ The original platform should not be considered available for use
